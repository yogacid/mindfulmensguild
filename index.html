What We Are

A small, tight-knit community of men committed to becoming better versions of themselves.

By conventional measures, the men here are doing fine. That's part of what makes it hard to talk about. Success answers a lot of questions and leaves the harder ones alone, and there aren't many places a man can say so out loud.

This is one of those places.

We meet once a month for two hours. We practice meditation or breathwork, and then we talk about what's actually going on. Nobody performs here. You get heard, and you get held to the kind of man you said you were trying to become.

Between calls we stay in touch. When something goes sideways for one of us, the others show up. Not out of obligation, but because by then you actually know each other.

A few times a year we get together in person. Being in the same room does something video calls can't.

Most of the men here are busy. Entrepreneurs, executives, creatives, fathers. None of that earns you anything in this room. What matters is whether you do the work, and whether the people around you can feel that you have.

The Guild's Code

Ten principles.
A compass for the journey.

Designed with our founding members, our code represents the principles we strive to live by. None of us are perfect. The code marks a direction to move toward, not a bar to clear.

Recognize that you are part of something larger than yourself. Your choices ripple outward into your family, your community, the world. Act accordingly.
Self-knowledge is the foundation of everything. Understand your patterns, your wounds, your gifts. Know where you stand in the web of relationships that sustain you.
Wholeness demands all three. The disciplined mind, the capable body, the cultivated spirit — each one serves the others. Don't neglect what is inconvenient to develop.
Do what you say. Say what you mean. And when you act, let compassion lead. Integrity is not about being perfect. It's about being honest when you're not.
There is something worth revering in every person, every place, every moment of genuine presence. Develop the eyes to see it, and the humility to honor it.
The man who can be seen — truly seen — is stronger than the man who hides. In this guild, you practice letting yourself be known. That takes more courage than most men ever find.
Your success is only as meaningful as what you do with it. Look for ways to give back to your community, your brothers, to life. Service is not optional; it is what gives everything else its weight.
Extremes are easy. Balance is hard. What looks like weakness is the work of holding contradictions without collapsing into one side. Discipline and ease. Ambition and contentment. Strength and softness.
Step outside. Slow down. The natural world has been teaching long before any of us arrived. Seasons, cycles, stillness: there is instruction in all of it, for those willing to be quiet enough to hear it.
There is no destination. The work continues for as long as you're alive. Stay curious. Stay humble. The moment you believe you've arrived is the moment you stop growing.
